The island sits six degrees south of the
equator. That proximity shapes everything:
temperatures hold between 25°C and 32°C
year-round, the ocean rarely drops below
25°C. Two short rainy seasons give way to
long dry periods that make this unique place
where increasing numbers of people choose
to live rather than merely visit.
In other words, Zanzibar is an established
destination reaching its moment.

In the tranquil heart of
a vibrant village
Paje sits on Zanzibar's south-east coast. Consistent
trade winds and a vast shallow lagoon stretching
almost two kilometres to the reef have made it
one of the world's premier kitesurf destinations.
That reputation drew the first wave of international
visitors. The restaurants, beach clubs, and villa
rentals that followed drew the second. The buyers
investing here today are the third.
More intimate than the north coast. More authentic.
More cosmopolitan — without being overrun.
Hola lies 500 metres from the beach. Close enough
to walk. Far enough from the tourist strip to feel like
somewhere you actually want to live.
Here you are not watching Paje from a distance.
You are part of it. A village within the village.
